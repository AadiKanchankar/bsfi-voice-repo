## Daily limits

The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is 25,000 rupees for a classic debit
card and 50,000 rupees for a platinum debit card. The default daily point of
sale and online limit is 50,000 rupees for classic and 200,000 rupees for
platinum.

## Changing a limit

A customer may raise or lower a limit through internet banking, through the
mobile application, or by asking this assistant. A request to raise a limit
requires successful identity verification and a one time password. A limit
reduction takes effect immediately; a limit increase takes effect after a
cooling period of 30 minutes.

## Fund transfer limits

The NEFT limit through internet banking is 10 lakh rupees per day. The RTGS
minimum is 2 lakh rupees per transaction. The IMPS limit is 5 lakh rupees per
day. The UPI limit is 1 lakh rupees per day, and 5 lakh rupees for verified
merchant categories.
