## Personal loan

The applicant must be between 21 and 60 years of age at maturity, with a
minimum net monthly income of 25,000 rupees for salaried applicants and a
minimum annual income of 5 lakh rupees for self employed applicants. The
minimum credit score is 700. Total monthly obligations after the new loan must
not exceed 55 percent of net monthly income.

## Home loan

The applicant must be between 21 and 70 years of age at maturity. Co applicants
are permitted and their income may be clubbed. The property must have a clear
and marketable title, and a legal and technical valuation is mandatory before
sanction.

## Documents required

Salaried applicants submit the last three salary slips, six months of bank
statements and the latest Form 16. Self employed applicants submit two years of
income tax returns with computation, audited financials where applicable, and
twelve months of bank statements.
