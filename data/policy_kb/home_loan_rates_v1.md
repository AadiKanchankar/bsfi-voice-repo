## Home loan interest rate slabs

Floating rate home loans are priced at the repo linked lending rate plus a
spread that depends on the loan amount and the applicant credit score. For
applications received between 1 April 2025 and 31 March 2026 the applicable
rates are 8.65 percent per annum for salaried applicants with a credit score of
750 and above, 8.90 percent per annum for salaried applicants with a score
between 700 and 749, and 9.15 percent per annum for self employed applicants.

## Loan amount and tenure

The minimum home loan amount is 5 lakh rupees and the maximum is 5 crore
rupees. Tenure runs from 5 years to 30 years, subject to the applicant
retirement age. The loan to value ratio is capped at 90 percent for loans up to
30 lakh rupees, 80 percent for loans between 30 lakh and 75 lakh rupees, and 75
percent above 75 lakh rupees.

## Processing fee

The processing fee is 0.35 percent of the sanctioned amount, with a minimum of
5,000 rupees and a maximum of 25,000 rupees, plus applicable taxes. The fee is
non refundable once the loan is sanctioned.
