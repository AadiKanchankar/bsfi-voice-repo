## Documents accepted as proof of identity and address

Officially valid documents are the passport, the driving licence, the Voter
identity card, the job card issued by NREGA signed by a State Government
officer, and the letter issued by the National Population Register. Aadhaar may
be used where the customer chooses to submit it voluntarily, and the number is
redacted in all records other than the mandated ones.

## Periodic updation

High risk customers are re-verified every two years, medium risk customers
every eight years and low risk customers every ten years. Where there is no
change in the customer details, a self declaration through a registered channel
is sufficient.

## Consequences of incomplete KYC

An account whose periodic updation is overdue is first restricted to credits
only. If the updation is not completed within the notice period, debits are
also restricted until the documents are furnished.
