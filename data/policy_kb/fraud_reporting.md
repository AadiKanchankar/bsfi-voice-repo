## Reporting a fraud

A suspected fraud must be reported immediately through the 24 hour helpline, at
any branch, or through this assistant. Every fraud report is handled by a human
officer; it is never closed by an automated system. The customer receives a
reference number at the point of reporting.

## Immediate steps taken

On receipt of a report the relevant card or channel is blocked, the disputed
transaction is marked under investigation, and the National Cybercrime
Reporting Portal reference is recorded where the customer has filed one.

## Resolution timeline

The bank completes its investigation within 90 days. Where the bank is liable,
the amount is credited within ten working days of the report, as a shadow
credit pending the outcome of the investigation.
