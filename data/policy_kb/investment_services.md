## Demat and trading account

A demat account holds shares and other securities in electronic form. Opening
one requires a PAN, an officially valid address document, a cancelled cheque
or bank statement, and a signature specimen. The account is usually active
within two working days of successful verification.

## Demat charges

Account opening is free. Annual maintenance is 300 rupees plus taxes, waived
for the first year and for Basic Services Demat Accounts holding under 50,000
rupees of securities. Brokerage on equity delivery is 0.25 percent of turnover
and on intraday is 0.03 percent, subject to a minimum of 20 rupees per
executed order.

## Mutual funds and systematic investment plans

Mutual funds can be bought as a lump sum or through a systematic investment
plan. The minimum systematic instalment is 500 rupees per month. An Equity
Linked Savings Scheme carries a lock in of three years from the date of each
instalment and qualifies for deduction under the applicable section of the
Income Tax Act. There is no lock in on an ordinary open ended equity fund, but
an exit load of 1 percent applies to redemptions within one year.

## Sovereign gold bonds

Sovereign gold bonds are issued in tranches announced by the Reserve Bank of
India. The minimum subscription is one gram and the maximum for an individual
is four kilograms per financial year. The tenor is eight years with an exit
option from the fifth year. Interest is 2.5 percent per annum on the nominal
value, paid half yearly.
