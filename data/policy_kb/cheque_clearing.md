## Clearing timelines

Local cheques presented in Cheque Truncation System clearing are credited on
the next working day. Outstation cheques drawn on a CTS grid location are also
credited on the next working day. Cheques drawn on locations outside the grid
are credited within seven working days.

## Cheque return charges

A cheque returned for insufficient funds attracts a charge of 500 rupees plus
taxes for the drawer. A cheque returned for a technical reason such as a
signature mismatch or a date error attracts a charge of 150 rupees plus taxes.
No charge applies where the return is attributable to the bank.

## Validity and stop payment

A cheque is valid for three months from the date of issue. A stop payment
instruction can be placed through internet banking or at a branch, and costs
100 rupees plus taxes per instrument. A stop payment cannot be placed on a
cheque that has already been presented and paid.
