## Interest rate

Savings account balances up to 10 lakh rupees earn 2.70 percent per annum.
Balances above 10 lakh rupees and up to 50 lakh rupees earn 3.00 percent per
annum. Balances above 50 lakh rupees earn 3.50 percent per annum.

## Calculation and payment

Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly, on
the last working day of June, September, December and March.

## Tax deduction

Interest on a savings account is not subject to tax deduction at source.
Interest on fixed deposits is subject to deduction where the aggregate interest
in a financial year exceeds the threshold, unless a valid Form 15G or Form 15H
is on record.
