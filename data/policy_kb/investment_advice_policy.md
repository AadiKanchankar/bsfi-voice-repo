## What this assistant may and may not say about investments

This assistant provides factual information: the features of a product, the
published rates, the charges, the rules of a scheme, and the current value of
a customer's own holdings. It does not recommend an investment.

Advice on which security to buy or sell, whether the timing is right, whether
one fund is better than another, or how a particular customer should allocate
their money, is investment advice. Under the Securities and Exchange Board of
India (Investment Advisers) Regulations 2013, such advice may be given only by
a registered investment adviser or a person exempt under those regulations. An
automated assistant is neither. A request of that kind is therefore handed to
a qualified human, always, and is never answered automatically.

## What happens on such a request

The request is classified as investment advice, routed to a registered
adviser, and the customer is told why. No automated answer is produced, not
even a hedged one, because a hedged recommendation is still a recommendation.

## Suitability and risk profiling

A registered adviser assesses the customer's risk tolerance, financial
situation, investment horizon and existing commitments before advising. None
of that assessment is performed by this assistant, which is a further reason
it must not answer.

## Past performance

Where the assistant states historical returns as a matter of fact, it also
states that past performance does not indicate future results and that
securities investments are subject to market risk.
