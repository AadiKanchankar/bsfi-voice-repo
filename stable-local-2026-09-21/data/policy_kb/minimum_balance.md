## Average monthly balance

A regular savings account in a metro or urban branch requires an average
monthly balance of 5,000 rupees. In a semi urban branch the requirement is
2,500 rupees and in a rural branch it is 1,000 rupees. A salary account and a
Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account carry no minimum balance requirement.

## Shortfall charges

Where the average monthly balance falls short, the charge is 6 percent of the
shortfall, subject to a minimum of 100 rupees and a maximum of 600 rupees per
month, plus taxes. The customer receives an alert before the charge is levied.

## Current accounts

A regular current account requires an average quarterly balance of 25,000
rupees. The shortfall charge is 1,500 rupees per quarter plus taxes.
