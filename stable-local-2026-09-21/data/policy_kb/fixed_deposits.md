## Interest rates for deposits below 3 crore rupees

Deposits of 7 to 45 days earn 3.50 percent per annum. Deposits of 46 to 179
days earn 5.75 percent. Deposits of 180 days to less than 1 year earn 6.50
percent. Deposits of 1 year to less than 2 years earn 7.10 percent. Deposits of
2 years to less than 5 years earn 7.00 percent. Deposits of 5 years and above
earn 6.75 percent.

## Senior citizen benefit

Resident senior citizens receive an additional 0.50 percent per annum on all
tenures for deposits below 3 crore rupees. Super senior citizens aged 80 and
above receive an additional 0.65 percent.

## Premature withdrawal

Premature withdrawal is permitted on most schemes. The interest paid is the
rate applicable for the period the deposit actually ran, less a penalty of 1.00
percent. Tax saver deposits with a five year lock in cannot be withdrawn
prematurely.
