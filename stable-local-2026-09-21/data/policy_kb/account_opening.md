## Account types

The bank offers a regular savings account, a salary account, a Basic Savings
Bank Deposit Account, a senior citizen savings account, a minor account and a
regular current account. The Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account has no minimum
balance requirement and carries four free cash withdrawals per month.

## Opening an account

An account may be opened at a branch or through the video KYC process on the
mobile application. Video KYC requires a PAN, an officially valid document and
a device with a working camera and microphone. The account is active within one
working day of successful verification.

## Nomination

Nomination is available and strongly recommended on every deposit account. A
nomination may be registered at account opening or at any time afterwards
through internet banking or at a branch, at no charge.
