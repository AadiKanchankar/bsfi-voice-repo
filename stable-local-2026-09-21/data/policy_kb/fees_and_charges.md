## Debit card charges

The annual maintenance charge is 200 rupees plus taxes for a classic debit card
and 500 rupees plus taxes for a platinum debit card. The first card issued with
a new account carries no issuance fee. A card replacement costs 200 rupees plus
taxes.

## ATM transaction charges

Five transactions per month at the bank own ATMs are free. At other bank ATMs,
three transactions per month are free in the six metro centres and five per
month elsewhere. Beyond the free limit, each financial transaction costs 23
rupees plus taxes and each non financial transaction costs 10 rupees plus
taxes.

## Fund transfer charges

NEFT and RTGS transactions initiated through internet banking or the mobile
application carry no charge. IMPS transactions up to 1,000 rupees carry no
charge; above that, the charge is 5 rupees plus taxes per transaction. UPI
transactions carry no charge to the customer.
