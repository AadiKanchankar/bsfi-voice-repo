## How to block a lost or stolen card

A card can be blocked through the mobile application, through internet banking,
by calling the 24 hour helpline, or by asking this assistant. Blocking takes
effect immediately and cannot be reversed. A blocked card cannot be unblocked;
a replacement card is issued instead.

## Liability for unauthorised transactions

Where a customer reports an unauthorised transaction within three working days
of receiving the communication, the customer bears zero liability. Reporting
between four and seven working days limits customer liability to the
transaction value or 10,000 rupees for a basic savings account and 25,000
rupees for other accounts, whichever is lower. Beyond seven working days,
liability is determined by the board approved policy.

## Replacement card

A replacement card is dispatched within seven working days to the registered
address. The replacement fee is 200 rupees plus taxes, and it is waived where
the block was raised because of a confirmed fraud.
