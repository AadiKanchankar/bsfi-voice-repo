## First level

A complaint may be raised at any branch, through the customer care helpline, or
through this assistant. Every complaint receives a ticket number immediately.
The bank responds within ten working days.

## Escalation levels

If the response is unsatisfactory or absent after ten working days, the
complaint escalates to the Nodal Officer at the Regional Office, who responds
within a further ten working days. The next level is the Principal Nodal
Officer at the Head Office.

## Banking Ombudsman

If the complaint remains unresolved thirty days after it was first raised, the
customer may approach the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through the
RBI complaint management system portal. The bank provides the complaint
reference and correspondence on request.
